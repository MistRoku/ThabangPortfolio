1. Overview

This policy explains what information this portfolio site collects and how it is used. The site is a static portfolio with a contact form. There are no accounts, no checkout, and no advertising profiles.

2. Information you provide

When you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, and message. This information is used only to read and respond to your enquiry. It is transmitted through the configured email service (EmailJS) and delivered to the site owner inbox.

3. Information collected automatically

The hosting platform may record standard technical logs such as pages requested, browser type, and approximate region for reliability and security. This portfolio does not run its own analytics tracker.

4. How information is used

  • To respond to messages sent through the contact form.
  • To keep the site secure and diagnose technical faults.
  • To meet legal obligations where required.

Contact details are never sold and are never added to a marketing list.

5. Sharing

Form submissions pass through the email delivery provider so the message can be sent. Apart from that delivery step and disclosures required by law, your information is not shared with third parties.

6. Data retention

Contact correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and maintain a reasonable record of professional correspondence. You can request deletion of your message by emailing thabangmokgonyana@gmail.com from the same address you used.

7. External links

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, live demos, and other external pages leave this site. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, so review those policies before providing them with personal information.

8. Children

This site is a professional portfolio and is not directed at children. The contact form should only be used by visitors able to enter into professional correspondence.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its contact handling changes. The revision date at the top shows the latest version.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests can be sent through the Contact page or by email to thabangmokgonyana@gmail.com.
